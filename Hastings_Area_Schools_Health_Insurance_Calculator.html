Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the 2027 caps-adjusted employee contributions from The Pool Hastings Area Schools Plan Illustration (1/1/2027). The district pays the 2027 PA 152 hard cap ($8,180.35 Single / $17,107.66 Two-Person / $22,310.11 Family per year); the employee pays the rest. Per-pay deductions are the annual cost divided by the number of pays selected above.
• Where the plan premium is below the hard cap, the employee cost is $0.00 and the difference is deposited into the employee's HSA. This is shown as a negative amount: Priority HSA 2500 PH01 – Single $213.83 and Family $4.02; Value HSA 3500 170 – Single $292.99 and Family $225.51.
• Benefits are from the BCBSM Benefits-at-a-Glance documents (Enhanced HSA 2000 121/122, Enhanced HSA 2000 175/176 and Value HSA 3500 170 effective 1/1/2025; Enhanced HSA 2500 210/211 effective 1/1/2026) and the Hastings Area Schools Priority Health PPO HDHP 2500 PH01 Schedule of Benefits effective 1/1/2027.
• The medical slider (Checkups → Major) sets office, online, urgent care and ER visits plus hospital/procedure charges per person; the Rx slider (None → Specialty) sets generic, preferred brand/specialty and non-preferred fills per person. Two-Person is modeled as 2 people and Full Family as 4. Typical charges: primary care $150, specialist $250, online visit $75, urgent care $350, ER $2,000. All five plans are HSA-qualified, so the full charge goes to the deductible, then coinsurance (0% on 121/122, 210/211 and PH01; 20% on 175/176 and 170) up to the out-of-pocket maximum. Hospital/procedure charges are treated the same way.
• Rx (30-day retail) is modeled at the stated copay per fill after the deductible, with no multiplier: Enhanced HSA 2000 121/122 and 175/176 – $10 / $80 / $100; Enhanced HSA 2500 210/211 – $20 / $80 / $100; Value HSA 3500 170 – $20 / $60 / $100; Priority HSA 2500 PH01 – $10 / $80 / $160. Where the plan uses brand coinsurance, the fill is modeled at the stated per-fill maximum, since a percentage of a typical brand-name drug cost reaches that maximum. Rx dollars paid also count toward the deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage (all five plans use aggregate family deductibles). Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum. Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included.
